    How to play

    Goal: don't hold the lowest card. Each player starts with 3 lives.

    • Each round, everyone gets one card face down.
    • In turn order, you either Keep your card or Swap it with the next player.
    • A player holding a King is protected — you can't force a swap onto them.
    • The last player each round (the dealer) swaps with the deck instead of a person.
    • After everyone has acted, cards are revealed. Lowest card loses a life — 2 is the lowest of the normal cards, but the Ace is worth just 1 and is the lowest card in the whole deck. You really don't want it!
    • The King is the highest card in the deck and can't be swapped onto you.
    • Lose all 3 lives and you're out. Last player standing wins the table.
    • After you Keep or Swap, a row of emoji appears — tap one to flood everyone's screen with it for a couple of seconds. Use it to celebrate, sulk, or bluff — nobody can tell if you're faking it! 😆
